JIT SaaS

Full Product & Functional Specification for Development
Product concept

JIT SaaS is a multi-tenant platform that allows individuals and organisations to create, operate and
grow their own branded training institute using JIT's infrastructure.
The important distinction is:
JIT is not a marketplace like Udemy where people simply upload courses.
A customer using JIT SaaS gets the infrastructure to operate their own school.
For example, someone could create:
PrimeTech Academy
Another customer could create:
Lagos School of Data
Another:
NextGen Cyber Academy
All three schools run on JIT SaaS, but their students experience them as separate institutes.
Each institute can have its own:

A useful internal principle for development is:

One platform. Many independent institutes.

Critical security requirement

An Organisation must NEVER be able to access another Organisation's private information.
If:
PrimeTech Academy = Organisation 23
and:
Lagos School of Data = Organisation 31
Organisation 23 cannot request:

   /api/organisation/31/students

and receive those Students.
The backend must reject it.

         Hiding the button on the frontend is not sufficient security.

1. DEFINITION OF A SUCCESSFUL
SAAS CONVERSION

The development team should use this as the ultimate test:
Imagine tomorrow a company called: ABC DIGITAL ACADEMY pays for JIT SaaS.

       Can ABC create its institute without JIT developers changing code?

       Can it upload its logo? Can it choose its colours?

       Can it create Courses? Can it set prices? Can it create Batches?

       Can it add Lecturers? Can it recruit Admission Specialists?

       Can Students register? Can Students pay? Can Admission Specialists
       earn Commission?

       Can Students receive their accounts? Can Lecturers teach them?

       Can Students submit Tasks? Can Lecturers grade them? Can attendance
       be recorded?

       Can ABC see its own revenue? Can ABC communicate with its Students?

       Can ABC do all of this without seeing Jorsas Institute's data?

       And can Jorsas Institute simultaneously continue operating normally?

If the answer is YES, then JIT has successfully become SaaS.
If the developers have to copy the application, create a new database, edit code, change logos
manually and deploy another version every time a new school joins, it is not yet the SaaS we are
describing.
